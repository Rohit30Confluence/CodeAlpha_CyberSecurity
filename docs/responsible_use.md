# Responsible-use guide

Use capture, inspection, and alerting only on systems and networks you own or where you have documented permission. Keep capture duration minimal, collect metadata rather than payloads, and protect logs as potentially sensitive security records.

The training scenarios are simulations. Do not send simulated phishing messages to people without organisational approval and a clear learning objective. The code-review findings are indicators requiring human verification, not proof of a vulnerability.

If an alert identifies suspicious activity, preserve relevant authorised logs, follow the organisation's incident-response process, and avoid retaliating, scanning, or accessing third-party systems.
